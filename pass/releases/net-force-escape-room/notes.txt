net-force-escape-room v2026.11.13b

Students can't copy the page any more (read aloud still works)
- Students can still highlight any text on the page, so text-to-speech keeps working: ChromeOS Select-to-Speak (highlight, then
  Search + S, or hold Search and click), Read&Write's Play button, and Chrome's Reading mode.
- What they can't do any more with the escape room's problems and pictures:
  - Copy or cut it (Ctrl + C, Ctrl + X, or Chrome's own Copy). If they paste anyway, all that comes out is "Copying is turned off
    for this assignment. Answer in your own words."
  - Right-click it. That menu is how a student would "Search Google for" highlighted words or use Google Lens without copying at all.
  - Drag highlighted words or a picture into the address bar or another tab.
- Inside an answer box nothing changes: students can copy and cut their own words, and right-click still shows spelling suggestions.
- New Settings row "Block copying from the page", right after "Block pasting into answers", checked to start. Uncheck it to let
  students copy again. It takes effect the next time a student loads the page.
- What it can't stop: a screenshot (Google Lens or "Search your screen"), a phone photo, a student retyping the question, or an AI side
  panel that reads the whole page. It makes copying take real effort, which is the goal.

The class website's escape room page blocks copying as soon as this release is published, even before this sheet is updated. Students
on school Chromebooks usually open the sheet's own page, though, so update this sheet to cover them: paste the new Code.gs and Index and
create the New version (Deploy > Manage deployments > pencil > New version > Deploy), or use Update now on the Assignment Launchpad.
Then run Self-Check > Set up / repair sheets once to add the new Settings row (until then copying is blocked anyway; the row is how you
turn it off). Nothing already in the sheet changes, and no new permission is needed.

Also from 2026.11.11d (optional), if this sheet skipped it:

- New: give full credit to an answer the key missed. In the Live Results tab, click the student's answer (a cell under Q1, Q2 ...),
  then Self-Check > Give full credit to the selected answers. The cell turns green and starts with [full credit], and the grade
  updates right away. Every change is in the Answer Log.
- Changing a cell's color does NOT give credit: Live Results is drawn again from the saved answers every time a student saves.
- Several at once: drag across cells, or hold Ctrl and click. Click a Q column's letter to give that question full credit for everyone
  who answered it. It asks first, and skips blank answers, pictures and drawings, and rows hidden by a filter (with one period showing,
  only that period gets it).

- The credit belongs to that answer. If the student changes it, it is checked against the answer key again (change it back and the
  credit comes back). A question with limited tries (choice, true/false, short, dropdown) takes no more tries once it has full credit.
- Late work: an answer that was there by the due date counts as on time.
- Self-Check > Take back full credit from the selected answers undoes it: the answer is checked against the key again.
- Recalculate all grades keeps those answers green. Classroom gets the new grade the next time grades upload (step 3, or the
  automatic upload).
- Tip: when many students used the same right words the key is missing, adding them to the key and running Recalculate all grades is
  still the faster fix.
- To use it: Update now on the Launchpad, or paste the new Code.gs and Index and make a New version. Then reload the sheet to see the

Also from 2026.11.11c, if this sheet skipped it:

  shows anyway), so a 5 MB photo is sent as about 200 KB. The size of the file in Drive no longer matters: there is no 8 MB limit any more

  kept copy and the sheet doesn't read Drive again for every class.

  After that, the same Chromebook skips the check for 30 days and goes straight to the assignment: one trip to the sheet instead of two, so a
  class of 36 opening together makes half as many trips. If the same Chromebook is back at the link within 10 minutes, the check runs again.
- If Google Drive turns a picture away on the sheet's own link (it can when a whole class asks for the same pictures at once), the picture
  now comes through the sheet instead of staying blank.
