- Fix: a student's drawing could disappear when they opened the assignment again. The drawing pad was blank for a few seconds while
  the saved drawing loaded, and a student who drew in that time started a new drawing that saved over the old one. Now the pad is
  covered ("Loading your saved drawing...") and takes no drawing until the saved one is back on it. If it can't load, the pad says so,
  keeps the saved drawing, and offers Try again or Draw a new one (which asks first).
- Fix: an answer could go missing when two saves reached the sheet in the same second (an answer checked right
  before the drawing saved, or while an autosave ran).
  The second save could read the student's work from before the first one and write the older copy back. Now each save is
  completely in the sheet before the next one starts. (Found on the October Sky self-check: an answer checked one second before
  the drawing saved came back blank.)
- Students who open this assignment through the class website (Class Pass) get the drawing pad fix as soon as it is published.
  The saving fix is in Code.gs, so it needs the paste and New version below.
- Older drawings are in your Google Drive trash for 30 days (each new save of a drawing puts the one before it there):
  open Trash and search the student's name.
- Your questions, answer keys, settings and student work are not touched.
