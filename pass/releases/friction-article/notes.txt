Optional update: wrong answers go straight to their hints (no "You're on the right track."), a faster Check and an obvious last try on limited tries questions, and a Shuffle question order setting.

- This page is built on the two-column page, so it gets the same changes:
- Wrong answers no longer start with "You're on the right track." A red typed answer goes straight to its score and hints (for example
  "Score: 0/2. Look at the cart after you press Go. ..."), so gibberish doesn't get praised. A wrong multiple choice pick shows just that choice's feedback ("That isn't the right
  answer." when it has none), and a short answer with a misconception starts with "Check this: ...".
- Faster Check on limited tries questions (choice, truefalse, short): the answer shows right away, like a typed answer's Check. The sheet
  checks the try without waiting in line, shows the result, then saves it in the background ("Checked. Saving..." then "Saved 6:54 PM").
  The try counts the moment it's checked, so it can't be used twice, and the right answer still never reaches the page until the question is done.
- The last try is obvious: a yellow warning right over the answer ("Last try! Read every choice carefully before you press Check."),
  "Last try!" next to the button, and the button says "Check (last try)". A true/false question (one try) shows "One try only! Read the
  statement carefully before you press Check." Wrong picks stay pink.
- New Settings row "Shuffle question order" (unchecked to start): checked = each student gets the questions in their own order, mixed
  within each blue section, so neighbors can't copy by number. Each student's questions are numbered 1, 2, 3 ... in their own order (the
  same order every time they open it); Live Results and the Question Summary keep the Questions tab's numbers. Answer choices were already
  shuffled for each student (Shuffle answer choices). Leave it unchecked for a lab whose questions go in order.
- To use it: paste the new Code.gs and Index, New version, then Self-Check > Set up / repair sheets (it adds the Shuffle question order row
  at the end of the Settings tab). For a practice set like the Kinetic Energy one, check that box.
- Students who open this assignment through the class website (Class Pass) see the last-try warning as soon as it is published. The faster
  Check, the new wording and the question shuffle need the paste and New version.
- Your questions, answer keys, settings and student work are not touched.

Also from 2026.11.06 (optional), if this sheet skipped it:
- This page is built on the two-column page, so it can use the new question types too. Its own questions don't change.
- New: three Answer types with a limited number of tries, for practice sets where students used to click choices until one turned green
  (like the Kinetic Energy Google Form): choice (multiple choice: the Choice columns and Right choice), truefalse (TRUE / FALSE: Right choice
  TRUE or FALSE), and short (a one-line answer checked by its Idea key words). A short answer whose key words are all numbers reads numbers:
  25, 25 J, 25J and 25.0 all count, and typing no number at all doesn't use a try.
- Tries: 2 for multiple choice, 1 for true/false, 3 for short answers (new Settings rows; a question's own Tries column changes it).
- Credit: right on try 1 = 100%, try 2 = 90%, try 3 = 80% (Settings: Credit by try). Tried but never right = 50% (Settings: Credit when
  never answered right). Never tried = 0. A question's Points still scale it. For a practice set, set Full credit at to 100%.
- After a wrong try the student sees the feedback for the choice they picked (the new Choice 1 feedback ... Choice 4 feedback columns),
  and opens a hint before trying again (Settings: Open a hint before trying again; uncheck it to let them retry right away).
- The light bulb at the top right of the question: Give me a clue, Show me an example, Watch a video (the Hint: clue, Hint: example and
  Hint: video or link columns). A question shows only the hints it has, and one with no hints has no light bulb. Hints never cost points.
- Choices are shuffled for each student and again after each wrong try (Settings: Shuffle answer choices). True/false is never shuffled.
- Students never see the right answer or the Explanation until the question is done (answered right, or out of tries). Then the right
  choice turns green and the Explanation shows in the black bar. The answer key still never reaches the page before that.
- Live Results shows each of these questions like "D. mass and velocity | right on try 2 | hint: clue" (green = right, yellow = tries left,
  red = out of tries). The Question Summary has a new column, % right on the first try. Recalculate all grades re-grades every try
  with today's key (so fixing a Right choice fixes the grades too).
- Self-Check > Check the Questions tab for problems warns about a choice question with too few choices or no matching Right choice, more than
  one right choice, a missing Explanation, a short answer with no key words, a Tries number that isn't 1 to 10, a hint link that isn't https,
  and feedback typed on the right choice.
- Typed answers with a blank Answer type work exactly as before (revise as many times as you like).
- To use it: paste the new Code.gs and Index, New version, then Self-Check > Set up / repair sheets. That adds seven Settings rows at the end of
  the Settings tab and the new Questions columns (Choice 1 to Choice 4 with their feedback, Right choice, Explanation, Tries, Hint: clue,
  Hint: example, Hint: video or link) at the end of the Questions tab. Nothing already in the sheet changes, and no new permission is needed.
- Students who open this assignment through the class website (Class Pass) get the new page as soon as it is published. A sheet with no
  choice, truefalse or short questions looks exactly the same.
- Your questions, answer keys, settings and student work are not touched.
