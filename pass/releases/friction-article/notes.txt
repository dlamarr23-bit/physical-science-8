Also from 2026.11.12b (optional), if this sheet skipped it:

Helping students get started
- Two new Questions tab columns: Sentence starters (one per line) and Word bank (commas between). They work on typed answers and short answers.
  Students see them as buttons right above the answer box: orange starters, blue words. A tap types it into the answer. A starter's ... is
  left off and its ___ is selected, so the student types right over it. Tapped ones get a check mark. Grading doesn't change: the answer
  still needs the right ideas to turn green. Leave both cells blank and nothing shows. Starters hide once the answer is green.
- Settings "Starters only after a wrong answer" (unchecked to start): checked = the starters and word bank stay hidden until that question's
  first Check comes back yellow or red.
- Settings "Say it button" (checked to start): every typed answer gets a Say it button in its black bar. The student talks, the words are
  typed into the answer, and they fix any word it got wrong before pressing Check. On the class website it listens right on the page (Chrome
  asks once to allow the microphone). On this sheet's own link (school Chromebooks) Google doesn't allow the microphone, so Say it opens a small
  Say it window from the class website that listens and sends the words back. Uncheck it to hide the button.

Small wins (Settings "Small wins", checked to start)
- A streak counter in the progress bar ("3 in a row!") for green answers in a row. A yellow or red Check ends the streak.
- A green +1 floats up from Check (with a little confetti) each time an answer turns green.
- Your wins, just above I'm finished (at the bottom of the questions side): a mystery picture that uncovers squares as answers turn green (every answer
  green = the whole picture; a small copy sits in the progress bar), and four badges: First Try (green on the first Check), Comeback (fixed
  a yellow or red answer), Hot Streak (5 green in a row) and Finisher (checked every question). A new badge shows a note at the bottom of the screen.
- Settings "Mystery picture": blank = a built-in science picture (each assignment gets its own: a roller coaster, a rocket launch, a volcano
  or Saturn). Or paste a Google Drive link (Share > Hart District: anyone with the link) or an https picture link: a funny photo works great.
  Type none for no picture (the streak, +1 and badges still show).
- Grades never change. The streak and badges are kept on the student's computer; the picture follows their green answers on any computer.
- Uncheck Small wins to turn all of it off.
