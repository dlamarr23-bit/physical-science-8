Also from 2026.11.11b, if this sheet skipped it:
LIVE LESSONS: questions reach every device on time, and the projector's video can't buffer.

• QUESTIONS ON EVERY SCREEN AT THE SAME MOMENT. Before, every student's device asked the sheet for the question in the same half second
  when the video stopped. Google only answers 30 of those at once for one teacher account, so in a class of more than about 28 the
  rest were turned away and got the question several seconds late. Now each device is handed the coming question a few seconds early,
  with the exact moment the projector will stop, and shows it by its own clock at that moment. Nobody sees a question before the video
  stops for it.
• A device whose check is turned away at a busy moment now tries again in about half a second (it used to wait 2 to 3 seconds).
• NO WAITING, AND THE VIDEO CAN'T STALL (Google Drive videos). Press Start live lesson as soon as you like. The video starts in a few
  seconds, as before. While it plays, the live lesson screen saves the rest of the video on your computer, well ahead of where the
  class is watching, and shows how far along it is. Once a part is saved it plays from your computer, not through the sheet, so 35
  devices using the sheet can't stall it. It stays saved, so later periods on that computer play from the saved copy.
• Students watching on their own (at home or in class) are not affected: their video starts in a few seconds and streams, as before.
• A regular Chrome window can save the video. A private or Incognito window often can't; the video then streams as before, and the
  screen says so.
• To update: paste the new Code.gs and Index (or Update now on the Launchpad), then Deploy > Manage deployments > pencil > New version.
  The live lesson screen and school Chromebooks use the sheet's own copy of the page, so the sheet needs the new version too.
