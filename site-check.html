Videos and links

Every night the site checks a few of the book's videos and web links, so each one is checked about once a week. When one stops working, the site looks for replacements and lists them here. Press Use this and students get the new one straight away. About once a week, if something new has stopped working, you get an email (it also lands in your Feedback inbox).

Who runs it: Cloudflare, the company that hosts this site, on the free plan. In your Cloudflare account, open Workers & Pages, then the Worker named "vocab-live", then Settings, then Trigger events. The job is the Cron Trigger "23 10 * * *", which means every day at 10:23 UTC (2:23 a.m. Pacific in winter, 3:23 in summer). Replacements come from one Brave search for each broken link; Cloudflare's AI picks the ones that suit an 8th grade class, and the site opens each one to make sure it works. The email is sent by your Class List sheet. The code is game-server/src/links.js in your vocabulary project on GitHub.

Free limits

Everything the site runs on is free up to a limit. This is how much of each one is used. When one runs out, that feature waits until it starts over; nothing is ever charged.
