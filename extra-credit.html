Science Extra Credit

Every book on this list has an AR test, and you can find it in the Placerita School Library or at the Santa Clarita Public Library. Read one, pass its AR test, and turn in a book form or a summary to earn the points shown on its cover.

How to earn your points

  1. 1
    Read a bookPick a book from the list below and check it out from the school library or the Santa Clarita Public Library. The “Where to find it” filter shows which library has it.
  2. 2
    Take the AR testTake the AR test for that book.
  3. 3
    Turn in a book form or a summaryFill out the Fiction or Nonfiction book form below, or type a one-page, double-spaced summary. Either one earns your points.

How many points is a book worth?

It depends on the book’s AR points: longer and harder books are worth more, and true (nonfiction) books get 5 extra. Every book on the list shows its points on its cover.

AR pointsFictionNonfiction
0.5 to 1.51520
2 to 4.52025
5 to 9.53035
10 to 14.54045
15 or more5050

You can earn up to 100 extra credit points each quarter.

Good to knowIt may sound like a lot of work, but the book also counts toward your AR points in English class. If there's a different science book you'd like to read that isn't on the list, you can ask me to approve it. Any questions? Come ask me for help.

Finished reading and passed the AR test? Pick the form that matches your book. Each question tells you what to write about, and there is a spot for a picture of your AR test.
