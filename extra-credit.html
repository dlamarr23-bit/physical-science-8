Science Extra Credit

Read a science book, pass its AR test, and turn in a book form. Every book on this list has an AR test, and you can find it in the Placerita School Library or at the Santa Clarita Public Library. Each book is worth 15 to 50 points.

How to earn your points

  1. PickChoose a book from the list below.
  2. ReadCheck it out from the school library or the Santa Clarita Public Library.
  3. TestTake and pass the book’s AR test.
  4. Turn inFill out the book form below, or type a one-page, double-spaced summary.
