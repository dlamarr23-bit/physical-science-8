The story

Where and when does the story take place?

Name the place and the time (a year, a season, the past or the future). Then describe one detail from the book that helped you picture it.

Try starting with: The story takes place in...

Who is the main character, and what are they like?

Give two words that describe the main character. For each word, tell something the character did in the book that shows it.

Try starting with: The main character is... One word that describes them is... because...

What kind of problem does the main character face?

Pick the one that fits best.

What is the problem, and why is it hard to solve?

Explain the main problem in the story and what makes it hard for the character.

Try starting with: The main problem is...

4

Retell the story

Tell what happens in your own words, in order. Together, these three answers are your summary.

Beginning: How does the story start?

Who do we meet, and what happens that gets the story going?

Try starting with: At the beginning of the story...

Middle: What are the most important events?

Tell two or three key events, including the most exciting part (the climax).

Try starting with: Next... Then... The most exciting part is when...

End: How does the story end?

How is the problem solved (or not solved)? How has the main character changed?

Try starting with: In the end...

5

Think about it

Where does science show up in this story?

Describe one part of the book that involves science, like energy, forces and motion, weather, space, technology, animals or the human body. Explain the science in your own words. Could it really happen?

Try starting with: In the book,... This connects to science because...

What lesson or message do you think the author wants readers to learn?

Write the lesson as a sentence, then tell which part of the story shows it.

Try starting with: The author wants readers to learn that...

How many stars would you give this book?

Would you recommend this book to a classmate? Why or why not?

Tell what you liked or didn't like, and what kind of reader would enjoy it.
